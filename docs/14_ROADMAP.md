# 14 — Roadmap

## v0.1 — Kernel
Identity/RLS, client brain, knowledge proposals, AI worker, meeting intelligence, marketing/content core, approvals, portal, calendar, Drive minimum.

## v0.2 — Operational agency layer
ProspectAccount/ProspectAudit conversion (if commercial module is desired), ServicePlan/ScopeEntitlements, Requests, Projects, Deliverables, Tasks, Change Requests, internal Inbox, better notifications/search and onboarding health.

## v0.3 — Connected measurement
GA4/Search Console/Google Business/Profile/social professional APIs where available; normalized Publication performance; analytics drill-down.

## v0.4 — Intelligence engine
Opportunity engine, competitor/reputation/visibility research, Content DNA, evidence-backed Insight proposals, experiments.

## v0.5 — Deterministic content automation
Brand tokens, template renderer, automated Tier-0 content generation, batch approval, richer DAM/asset metadata.

## v0.6 — Distribution automation
Official social publishing integrations, retry/idempotency, publication health, compliance gates.

## v1 — Mature Marketing OS
Capacity planning, advanced automation builder, richer integrations, asset rights, advanced observability, stronger analytics/experiments, client reporting automation.

## Future ideas, not commitments
- image/video generative pipelines;
- agency-wide anonymized learning;
- external SaaS for agencies;
- fine-tuned models after sufficient approved corpus.
